# Sunflowers

Because someone once told me sunflowers are always given in pairs.

A sketchbook of fourteen photographs. Each photo sits on the left page, and a hand-drawn ink-and-wash sketch draws itself on the right. It opens on a cover with two sunflowers and ends with a note.

Live at **https://dhruwayush.github.io/NeelamDon/**

## Features
- Cover and last page with a sunflower pair
- Compare: drag across a sketch to wipe between the photo and the drawing
- Doodle on any page (saved in your browser)
- Swipe, arrow keys, or the contact sheet to turn pages
- Lofi piano played live with the Web Audio API

## Editing
- `index.html`: layout, navigation, sketch engine, page 1 sketch, captions (`sketchbookData`), and the cover and last-page text (`coverPage`, `endPage`)
- `pages.js`: sketches for pages 2–14 and the two sunflower pages
- `photos/`, `thumbs/`: WebP images · `og.jpg`: link-preview image
